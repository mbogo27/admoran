## Pricing models

**One-off audit.** A fixed-fee diagnosis: a full technical, on-page, and (where relevant) local review, delivered as a prioritised findings document. No ongoing commitment. Good for a first engagement, or for checking a claim before signing anything larger. See [what an SEO audit actually involves](/guides/seo-audit) for what the deliverable should contain.

**Project.** A defined, scoped piece of work with a start and an end: a site migration, a batch of technical fixes, a content build for a specific set of pages. Priced against the scope, not the calendar.

**Monthly retainer.** Ongoing work, billed monthly, for sites and markets that shift enough to need continuous attention rather than a single fix.

**Performance-based.** Pricing tied to rankings or traffic hit. This is usually a warning sign, not a saving — the incentives it creates are covered in [SEO red flags](/guides/seo-red-flags), and a provider confident in their own work rarely needs to structure pricing this way to prove it.

## What drives the range

| Driver | Effect on price |
|---|---|
| Site size | More pages and templates mean more to audit and fix |
| Competition | A crowded sector needs more sustained work to hold position |
| Current technical state | A site with years of unresolved issues costs more to bring up to a clean baseline |
| Content production | Included content raises the fee; audit-and-fix-only engagements cost less |

A quote at the low end of a range for a large, technically troubled site in a competitive sector is a mismatch worth asking about directly.

## What a low quote usually means

A quote well below the range for the stated scope usually means one of three things: the scope is narrower than it looks, the deliverable is templated rather than a genuine diagnosis of this specific site, or the work relies on shortcuts — bought links, thin content at volume — that carry the risks covered in [SEO red flags](/guides/seo-red-flags). None of the three is disclosed upfront; all three show up in the deliverable if asked about directly.
